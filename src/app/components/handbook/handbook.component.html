Core Loop

  1. Select a creature in the Evolution Tree.
  2. Add up to three unlocked creatures to Squad.
  3. Start an Arena auto-battle.
  4. Earn XP, Coins, DNA Shards, and possible items.
  5. Spend rewards to unlock evolutions.
  6. Use Collection filters to choose the next chase target.

Rewards

Wins grant +120 Coins, +8 DNA Shards, +35 XP, one battle win, and a 25% item-drop chance.

Losses still grant +30 Coins, +2 DNA Shards, and +12 XP.

Evolution

Evolution requires an unlocked source creature, enough level, Coins, DNA Shards, and sometimes an item.

When evolution succeeds, the target unlocks, resources are spent, and the new form becomes selected.

Squad Power

Team Power is HP + Attack + Defense + Speed for every squad member.

A full squad is usually stronger than one rare creature alone.
