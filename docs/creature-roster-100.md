## Build Archetype Seeds

- **Bloomline:** Nature sustain, shielded growth, long fights, item chase through `Bloom Fuse`.
- **Overheatline:** Fire burst, first-strike pressure, reward spikes through `Ignition Coil`.
- **Tideguard:** Water control, HP recovery, safe battle farming through `Tide Pearl`.
- **Nullline:** Dark tempo, crit-risk payoffs, late gates through `Shadow Gem` and `Null Circuit`.
- **Prismline:** Light speed, crit protection, clean Special chases through `Solar Crest` and `Prism Key`.
- **Forgeframe:** Machine utility, item drop hooks, defensive win consistency through `Ancient Gear`.
- **Wildcore:** Beast bruisers, high HP, arena trophy routes through `Beast Sigil`.
- **Blightloop:** Toxic pressure, poison-stall flavor, risky Special routes through `Toxic Vial`.
